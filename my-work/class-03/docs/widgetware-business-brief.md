# WidgetWare Business Brief

## 1. What WidgetWare Sells
WidgetWare offers software and services designed to help manufacturing and industrial-automation companies modernize their plant operations and implement governed, high-value AI capabilities.

Our primary products:
* **Plant Operations Platform**: Connects plant-operational information and helps teams monitor and improve manufacturing processes.
* **Industrial AI Accelerator**: Helps industrial organizations identify and implement governed AI use cases.

## 2. Target Audience (Who We Sell To)
We target decision-makers in manufacturing and industrial automation environments, including:
* **Plant Operations Platform Buyers**:
  * VP of Manufacturing
  * Plant Operations Director
  * Chief Digital Officer (CDO)
* **Industrial AI Accelerator Buyers**:
  * Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
  * VP of Digital Transformation
  * Head of Industrial AI

## 3. Plausible Fit (Ideal Customer Profile)
An account is a plausible fit for WidgetWare if it meets the following criteria:
* **Company Size**: Minimum of 5,000 employees.
* **Preferred Industries**: Manufacturing, Industrial Automation, Automotive Manufacturing, Electronics Manufacturing, and Industrial Equipment.
* **Preferred Regions**: United States, Europe, and India.
* **Excluded Industries**: Consumer Retail, Restaurants, and Personal Services.

## 4. Key Buying Signals
We actively monitor and leverage the following signals:
* New AI leadership appointments
* Active digital transformation programs
* Plant modernization projects
* Active hiring for GenAI positions
* Launch of manufacturing data initiatives

## 5. SDR Process Outputs
The SDR research process must produce a structured context object that:
* Evaluates the target account against the ICP rules.
* Separates information into five distinct layers (System Instructions, Business Context, Task Context, Retrieved Evidence, and Workflow State).
* Preserves source provenance for every factual evidence record.
* Separates verified facts from inferences, derived facts, and unknown information.

## 6. Human Approval Boundary
The future SDR agent must act purely as an analysis tool and stop before any external execution. Explicit human approval is required for:
* Any external outreach (e.g., sending emails or social messages).
* Any CRM writes or modifications.
* Making any pricing commitments or contractual statements.
